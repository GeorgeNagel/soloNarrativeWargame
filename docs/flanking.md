# Flanking

- **Arcs**: A hex has 6 faces. The unit's facing edge plus its two adjacent
  edges (3 total) are its **front**; the remaining 3 are its **rear**.
- **Effect**: A unit engaging an enemy through that enemy's rear arc inflicts
  **double** the hits it rolled. See `docs/combat.md` for where the doubling
  falls in the order of modifiers.
- The book distinguishes Front, Left Flank, Right Flank and Rear on a
  rectangular base, and treats flank and rear alike for this purpose. The two
  arcs above are the hex equivalent.
