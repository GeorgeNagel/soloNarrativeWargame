# Shooting

Adapted from the Shooting procedure in Neil Thomas, *One-Hour Wargames*, ch. 3.

- **Who shoots**: Archers and Skirmishers only.
- **Field of fire**: a unit may shoot at a single target within **45°** either
  side of its faced edge. The cone is measured as a real bearing from hex centre
  to hex centre, so it widens with distance rather than snapping to the six hex
  directions.
- **Range**: 4 hexes — the book's 12" at 3" per hex.
- **Moving and shooting**: a unit may not shoot in a round in which it advanced
  — giving up ground is the whole of the round's cost, and it buys no volley.
  Wheeling is not moving, so a unit may turn onto a target, before or after an
  advance it never takes, and still loose. An advance refused for a held hex
  still counts as moving, and still costs the shot; an advance refused because
  melee has hold of the unit does not, since the unit never moved.
- **Target**: the nearest enemy in range and inside the cone, measured where the
  round's movement leaves the shooter, ties broken by roster order. There is no
  shoot order — as with contact, the choice is made by where the unit ends up
  pointed and whether it spent the round advancing.

## Assessing casualties

The shooter rolls one **d6** and applies its type's modifier. The result is the
number of hits the target acquires:

| Type | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Archers | — |
| Skirmishers | −2 |

That result is then halved if the target is **armoured** (Infantry), with the
fraction rounded **in favour of the unit shooting**. There is no rear bonus for
shooting — the book gives that to hand-to-hand combat alone.

The book also halves hits against a target in **cover** (woods or towns). The
board carries no terrain yet, so that modifier is not implemented.

## Timing

Shooting resolves once a round, after every unit has finished moving and
**before** the melee, following the book's sequence of play. Hits from shooting
and from melee land together, and elimination is checked once both have been
applied — so a unit shot past 15 hits still fights its melee that round, exactly
as the book eliminates units in a later step than either.

Units do not block line of sight. The book has no such rule for shooting, and
its interpenetration rule governs movement only.
