# Shooting

Adapted from the Shooting procedure in Neil Thomas, *One-Hour Wargames*, ch. 3.

- **Who shoots**: Archers and Skirmishers only.
- **Field of fire**: a unit may shoot at a single target within **45°** either
  side of its faced edge. The cone is measured as a real bearing from hex centre
  to hex centre, so it widens with distance rather than snapping to the six hex
  directions.
- **Range**: 4 hexes — the book's 12" at 3" per hex.
- **Moving and shooting**: a unit may not shoot in a tick in which it advanced.
  Wheeling is not moving, so a unit may turn onto a target and still loose in
  the same tick. An advance that is refused for a held hex still counts as
  moving, and still costs the shot.
- **Target**: the nearest enemy in range and inside the cone, ties broken by
  roster order. There is no shoot order — as with contact, the choice is made by
  where the unit is pointed and whether it spends the tick advancing.

## Assessing casualties

The shooter rolls one **d6** and applies its type's modifier. The result is the
number of hits the target acquires:

| Type | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Archers | — |
| Skirmishers | −2 |

That result is then halved if the target is **armoured** (Infantry), with the
fraction rounded **in favour of the unit shooting**. There is no rear bonus for
shooting — the book gives that to hand-to-hand combat alone.

The book also halves hits against a target in **cover** (woods or towns). The
board carries no terrain yet, so that modifier is not implemented.

## Timing

Shooting resolves at each tick boundary, **before** the melee, following the
book's sequence of play. Hits from shooting and from melee land together, and
elimination is checked once both have been applied — so a unit shot past 15
hits still fights its melee that tick, exactly as the book eliminates units in
a later step than either.

Units do not block line of sight. The book has no such rule for shooting, and
its interpenetration rule governs movement only.
