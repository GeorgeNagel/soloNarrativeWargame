# Temple Generator

Low-magic, dark-ages world. Magic is almost always evil and corrupting.

Roll **Founded** first, then follow the tables for that era.

## Background

- **Old Ones:** dark gods who roamed the earth before humans.
- **Early humans:** placed on earth by the Old Ones as slaves, to farm and burn sacrifices whose smoke fed the gods.
- **First civilizations:** rose under the new, incorporeal gods. Many faiths.
- **Cataclysm:** the new gods warred with the Old Ones. Earthquakes, volcanoes and tsunamis followed. The Old Ones were buried, imprisoned or killed.
- **Second civilizations:** descendants and remnants. No pre-cataclysm city is continuously inhabited.
